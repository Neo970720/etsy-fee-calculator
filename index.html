How Etsy's fees work in 2026

Etsy does not charge one fee. On a normal sale it charges up to five, and only the first two are the same in every country. This calculator uses the rates published on Etsy's own help pages on 2026-11-20; the full table, with sources, is in the paid workbook's Fee Watch sheet.

FeeWhat it is charged on2026 rate

Currency conversionSale amount, if your payout account is in another currency2.5%

Two lines most sellers miss: payment processing is charged on the sales tax as well as the order, and Etsy adds VAT to its own fees in most countries. Both are included above.

Questions

Why is the fee higher than 6.5% + 3% + $0.25?

Because the transaction fee and the processing fee are charged on different bases, and both are percentages of a total that includes shipping. On a $20 item with $5 shipping you pay $0.20 listing, $1.63 transaction and $1.00 processing — $2.83, or 11.3% of what the buyer paid.

My shop is outside the US — does this tool cover it?

Yes, all 32 Etsy seller countries. Pick your country and the payment processing rate, the regulatory fee and the VAT on fees all switch. Australia, Canada and New Zealand also have a higher rate on international orders, which the calculator applies when you mark the order as international.

How do I know the rates are current?

They were read from Etsy's payment processing, regulatory fee, Offsite Ads and currency conversion help pages on 20 November 2026, including the 22 June 2026 regulatory fee revisions. Etsy can change them at any time — your payment account statement is always the final word.

Does the shipping I charge count as revenue?

Yes, and it is feeable. Etsy charges its transaction fee on shipping, so "free shipping" you absorb is a real cost on top of the label.

What about refunds?

Not modelled here. Etsy credits the processing fee proportionally when you refund, so a refund is not simply negative revenue.
